# Review round 9 — Associate Editor, *Bioresource Technology*

## Assessment
| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Scope | Within scope: data-driven modelling of biomass hydrothermal conversion; the journal publishes many ML surrogates of this kind (20 PubMed-indexed papers), including the direct predecessor. Risk: reviewers from process engineering may view it as "statistics paper" — mitigated by the domain discussion (work-up conventions, feedstock families, solvothermal media, recommendations for practitioners and curators). |
| Novelty | Moderate: an evaluation/methodology paper with a negative domain hypothesis and a validated procedure assembled from known components. Clearly differentiated from Elfetni et al. (2027). |
| Technical quality | High: locked test, pre-registration, study-level statistics, multiple shifts, duplicate audit, open code. |
| Evidence ↔ claims | Claims are generated from result files; overstatements corrected in rounds 1, 5 and 8. |
| Reproducibility | Single command; data vendored (CC BY); unit tests. |
| Practical significance | Clear recommendations; honest that intervals are wide (±30 wt% for a new study). |
